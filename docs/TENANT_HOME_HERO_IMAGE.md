## Prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural
Asset type: wide photographic header background for the 'Chỗ ở của tôi' (My home) screen of a Vietnamese rental housing mobile app.
Primary request: Create a distinct inviting lived-in rental studio interior, conveying a comfortable current home and everyday living. Landscape 16:9 composition, ideally 1672x941 or similar.
Scene/backdrop: a tidy modest modern Vietnamese studio with a soft warm off-white wall and pale sage accents. On the right half, a neatly made small bed with cream bedding and a sage throw, a compact oak bedside table, a warm lamp, and a leafy potted plant beside a sunlit window with sheer linen curtains. Natural materials and believable scale, attractive but attainable student/young-professional accommodation, no luxury hotel aesthetic.
Style/medium: realistic high quality interior photography, natural soft daylight, warm wood and fresh muted greens.
Composition/framing: wide eye-level view, all distinct furnishings mostly in the right 50 percent. The left 50 percent should be quiet pale wall with subtle natural light and minimal visual detail, usable as negative space for dark green app title and subtitle rendered separately. Ensure the bed, plant and interior still read clearly when cropped to a wide mobile banner of about 400x220, aligned center-right. Main objects within the middle vertical 70 percent, no extreme closeup.
Constraints: image only, no letters, no text, no logos, no watermark, no people, no apartment-building exterior, no cityscape, no UI elements.
