# Ảnh đầu trang Tin nhắn và Thông báo

Tạo bằng công cụ image_gen tích hợp (built-in), nền không trong suốt. Hai ảnh được lưu trong dự án và khai báo trong pubspec.yaml.

- `assets/images/tenant_messages_hero.png`: điện thoại và bong bóng hội thoại.
- `assets/images/tenant_announcements_hero.png`: chuông, phong thư và thẻ thông báo.

Bố cục chừa khoảng trống bên trái và phía trên cho tiêu đề cùng thanh tài khoản; chữ được dựng bằng Flutter. Ảnh gốc được giữ nguyên tại nơi công cụ tạo ảnh lưu trữ.

## Prompt Tin nhắn

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production background image for the full-width top hero of a Vietnamese rental housing mobile app, Messages section. Create one beautiful wide 16:9 landscape image, no UI mockup, no words. Premium photorealistic 3D editorial still life in a sunny modern rental apartment: on the RIGHT side, an elegant ivory smartphone leaning slightly on a pale natural oak desk, with two sculptural rounded emerald-green and white speech bubbles floating beside its upper half. The phone screen has only simple blank rounded green conversation shapes, absolutely no letters or numbers. A small leafy houseplant, soft ivory curtains, subtle modern apartment window in the distant background. LEFT 55 percent of frame is calm clean pale mint and warm-white negative space for dark green text added later in code; TOP 25 percent also airy and uncluttered for an app header. Main composition lives at x=72-85 percent, y=55-65 percent, with comfortable space around it so it survives mobile cover cropping. Soft morning daylight, tasteful soft shadows, tactile matte ceramic and frosted glass, realistic oak wood, minimal elegant decor. Palette: sage, pale mint, ivory, natural light wood, emerald #08A94F accents; harmonious with a bright photo of a contemporary white apartment building and greenery. Polished inviting calm mood, no people, no logos, no watermark, no typography, no border, no rendered app chrome. Single finished background illustration, not a collage.
